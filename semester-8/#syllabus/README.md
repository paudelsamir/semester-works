## Elective Subjects (Choose one)

### 4. Distributed Networking (CSC464)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Overview of distributed networking model, client server model, communication models, internetworking, interprocess communication, fault tolerance, reliability, replication, security issues and new developments in distributed networking.

---

### 5. Game Technology (CSC465)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Practical and conceptual introduction to game design and development including basic ideas of game design, learn to design a game, working as a game designer. Main platform: Unity.

---

### 6. Distributed and Object-Oriented Database (CSC466)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Concepts of distributed and object oriented database management systems. Focus on DDBMS, distributed database design, distributed query processing, distributed concurrency control, concepts of OODBMS, language and design of object oriented database.

---

### 7. Introduction to Cloud Computing (CSC467)

| Unit | Title | Hours |
|------|-------|-------|

| 2 | Cloud Computing Architecture | 6 |
| 3 | Cloud Virtualization Technology | 10 |
| 4 | Cloud Programming Models | 12 |
| 5 | Cloud Security | 6 |
| 6 | Cloud Platforms and Applications | 12 |

**Text Books:**
- Dr. Kumar Saurabh, *Cloud Computing*
- Raj Kumar Buyya, Christian Vecchiola, S. Thamarai Selvi, *Mastering Cloud Computing*

---

### 8. Geographical Information System (CSC468)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Spatial data structure, modeling and database design, different techniques for capturing the real world, spatial data manipulation, analysis and visualization, spatial data infrastructure and data standardization, overview of open GIS and open source GIS data.

---

### 9. Decision Support System and Expert System (CSC469)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Study uses of artificial intelligence in business decision making. Emphasis on business decision making process, design and development of decision support systems and expert systems.

---

### 10. Mobile Application Development (CSC470)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Mobile application development frameworks, architectures, design and engineering issues, techniques, methodologies for mobile application development.

---

### 11. Real Time Systems (CSC471)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Different concepts of real time systems mainly focusing on scheduling, access control, memory management, optimization, and real time communications.

---

### 12. Network and System Administration (CSC472)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

Network and system administration including initial installation of OS to day-to-day administrative tasks such as Network and Server Configurations, management of user accounts and disk space, and troubleshooting.

---

### 13. Embedded Systems Programming (CSC473)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory + Lab | **Full Marks:** 60 + 20 + 20

ARM based embedded system overview — assembly level programming, efficient C programming and embedded OS.

---

### 14. International Business Management (MGT474)

**Credits:** 3 | **Nature:** Theory | **Full Marks:** 80 + 20

Globalization and international business, global economy and regional, international trade and investment theories and practices, national difference in political, economic and socio-cultural environment, strategies for international business and international financial environment.
